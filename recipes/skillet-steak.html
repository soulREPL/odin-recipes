Cast-Iron-Skillet Steak Recipe

How to Make the Best Cast-Iron-Skillet Steak Ever

You can enjoy a perfectly seared cast-iron steak any night of week at home. Read on to learn how to cook steak in a skillet in just about 10 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 2 ribeye steaks
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper

Directions

  1. Preheat the cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. Season the steaks with salt and pepper.
  3. Drizzle olive oil into the skillet.
  4. Place the steaks in the skillet and cook for 4-5 minutes on each side for medium-rare.
  5. Let the steaks rest for 5 minutes before serving.
